Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Fluent 625 ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the app:

  • Email address — used to create and manage your account.
  • Study progress data — which words you've studied, your ratings, session history, and streaks. This is stored to power the spaced repetition algorithm.
  • Images you upload — photos you choose to associate with vocabulary words. These are stored privately and only visible to you.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Fluent 625 learning experience.
  • To sync your progress across devices.
  • To send daily study reminders, if you enable them.

We Do Not

  • Sell your data to third parties.
  • Use your data for advertising.
  • Share your information with anyone except the service providers needed to run the app (listed below).

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — database and file storage. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. Privacy Policy
  • Apple — payment processing for in-app purchases. Privacy Policy

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete all your progress at any time from the Settings screen in the app. To permanently delete your account and all associated data, contact us at support@dishaa.net.

Children's Privacy

Fluent 625 is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
